# Projects

## Landscape Design — PNW Driveway & Entry

A complete landscape design for a Pacific Northwest property on Discovery Bay, Jefferson County, WA. Designed in Twinmotion with a focus on native plantings, natural stone placement, and low-maintenance Pacific Northwest aesthetics.

### Design Philosophy
- Cliff-based density gradient for driveway stone placement
- Native PNW plantings (Oregon grape, sword fern, salal)
- Angular mossy basalt with partial burial for natural appearance
- Minimal disturbance to existing old-growth canopy
